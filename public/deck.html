The Problem

Prediction markets are intimidating

Existing platforms require deep financial knowledge, complex order books, and feel like Bloomberg terminals. New users bounce in 30 seconds. There's no reason to come back tomorrow.

Complex UI

Order books, limit orders, slippage — overwhelming for beginners.

No Retention

Trade once, never return. Zero reason to build a habit.

Isolated

No social features, no competition, no community around trading.

03 / 08
The Solution

StreakTrader — Trading meets gaming

One-tap UP/DOWN predictions with streak multipliers, shields, and social competition. Think Duolingo's engagement loops applied to prediction markets.

🎯 One-Tap Trading

Pick UP or DOWN. Set stake. Done. No order books.

🔥 Streak Multiplier

Win consecutive trades. Multiplier grows. Payouts increase.

🛡️ Streak Shield

Protect your streak from one loss. Free shields daily.

🧠 AI Copilot

AI-powered signals with confidence scores and risk gates.

📊 Prediction Score

0-100 skill rating based on win rate, streak, and consistency.

🎴 Shareable Cards

One-tap export your streak as a beautiful image for Twitter.

06 / 08
Technical

Under the hood

Smart Contracts (Solidity 0.8.24)

  • StreakRegistry — Records trades with streak tracking, best streak, win rate
  • ShieldManager — Daily shield claims with cooldown timer
  • ChallengeArena — P2P challenges with stakes and time limits
  • ScoreOracle — On-chain score computation (0-100)
  • 21 unit tests across all contracts

SDK Integration

  • DreamDEX Markets SDK — Real market discovery, order placement
  • Binance API — Live BTC price during settlement
  • MetaMask — Wallet connection, network switching, on-chain recording
  • Server APIs — Leaderboard sync, streak persistence, trade recording
  • AI Copilot — Signal analysis with risk gates
