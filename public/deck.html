The Problem

Prediction markets are too complex for normal people

Existing platforms require understanding order books, limit orders, slippage, and financial terminology. New users bounce in 30 seconds. There is no habit loop, no social competition, no reason to come back tomorrow.

Too Complex

Order books, limit orders, slippage, financial jargon. Overwhelming for beginners.

No Retention

Trade once, forget forever. Zero habit loop. No reason to return tomorrow.

Isolated

No social features, no competition, no community. Trading alone is boring.

03 / 09
The Solution

One-tap trading meets gamification

Pick UP or DOWN on live BTC and ETH markets. Win consecutive trades to grow your streak multiplier. The same engagement loop that made Duolingo addictive, applied to on-chain prediction markets.

One-Tap Trading

Pick UP or DOWN. Set stake. Confirm. Done. Ten seconds.

Streak Multiplier

Win consecutive trades. Multiplier grows from 1x to 10x. Earn more.

Streak Shield

Protect your streak from one loss. Free daily. Max 3 stored.

AI Copilot

Real Binance price analysis. Momentum, volatility, probability signals.

Challenges

Head-to-head trades against friends on the same market.

Social Feed

See other traders' wins, losses, and streaks in real-time.

Tournaments

Weekly competitions with rankings and prizes.

Streak Betting

Bet on whether other traders keep their streaks.

Shareable Cards

One-tap export streak as beautiful PNG for Twitter.

Prediction Score

0-100 skill rating on-chain based on win rate and consistency.

06 / 09
Technical

Under the hood

Smart Contracts

  • StreakRegistry — Records trades, streak, best streak, win rate per wallet
  • ShieldManager — Daily shield claims, activation, cooldown timer
  • ChallengeArena — P2P challenges with stakes and time limits
  • ScoreOracle — On-chain score computation (0-100)
  • All deployed on Shannon Testnet (Chain 50312)

SDK Integration

  • DreamDEX Markets SDK — Real market discovery and order execution
  • Binance API — Live BTC price during settlement
  • MetaMask — Wallet connection, network auto-switch
  • Vercel KV — Persistent leaderboard and streak storage
  • AI Copilot — Momentum, volatility, probability analysis
